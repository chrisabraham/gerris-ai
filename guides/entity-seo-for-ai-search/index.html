What is entity SEO, and why does it matter for AI search?

Entity SEO is the practice of making it easier for search systems to recognize which real-world organization, person, product, or place a page describes. It is about identity and relationships, not merely repeating a keyword.

Published November 25, 2026. Written by Chris Abraham, Gerris.

What is an entity?

An entity is a distinguishable thing: a company, person, product, location, or concept. Two companies may share a name but remain different entities. A brand can have a legal company, trading name, founder, and multiple locations. Accurate entity descriptions help systems avoid merging those records.

What facts form the canonical identity?

Document the official name, known trading names, domain, physical or service-area details, contact information, leadership, products, and relevant registrations. Publish the same current facts on your website and on profiles you control. For businesses without a public storefront, do not invent an address to satisfy a template.

How do external sources support recognition?

Independent directories, professional associations, government registries, legitimate news coverage, and verified profiles can corroborate claims. Their value depends on relevance and reliability, not on a raw backlink count. Correct outdated records and avoid creating fake profiles merely to multiply mentions.

How should Organization and Person schema be used?

Describe the organization and its people with valid schema.org properties and stable @id values. Link related entities consistently and keep the JSON-LD aligned with visible content. Structured data helps machines parse declared facts; it does not make unsupported claims true or guarantee a knowledge panel.

What does sameAs mean?

Use sameAs only for URLs that identify the same entity, such as an official organization profile. A customer's website, unrelated article, or sister company is not automatically the same entity. Misusing sameAs can introduce ambiguity rather than resolve it.

How do you handle name collisions?

Search for the company name alongside its industry and city, then inspect competing entities. Add distinguishing details naturally to the about page and relevant profiles: specialization, location, founder, and official domain. Where appropriate, document prior names and acquisitions clearly instead of erasing history.

How does local entity work differ?

For local businesses, check Google Business Profile eligibility, primary category, hours, address or service area, and genuine reviews. Keep directory information consistent while respecting platform rules. A service-area business should not fabricate a storefront, and a multi-location brand should distinguish real locations from virtual pages.

Can you force a Knowledge Panel or AI mention?

No. Search platforms decide which entities to recognize and which information to display. You can improve source accuracy and reduce ambiguity, but a Knowledge Panel is not a purchased deliverable and an AI recommendation is not an entitlement.

How do you audit an entity?

Create a source inventory with URL, claimed name, category, address or service area, contact details, and last verified date. Flag contradictions by severity. Fix official sources first, then high-trust third-party records, and document requests that remain pending.

What does success look like?

Fewer incorrect facts, fewer merged identities, consistent service descriptions, better discovery for relevant queries, and more accurate citations. Track actual observations rather than declaring success because schema validation passed.
