Why doesn't ChatGPT recommend my business?

ChatGPT can recommend competitors while omitting a perfectly legitimate business. The omission is not proof of a penalty. The answer depends on the question, the model, whether web search is used, the sources retrieved, and the evidence available about each candidate.

Published November 25, 2026. Written by Chris Abraham, Gerris.

Is ChatGPT searching the web or answering from its model?

Start by establishing the mode. A response based on a model's existing knowledge is not the same test as a response that searches current web pages. Record the product, model if visible, whether search was enabled, date, location context, and the exact wording of the prompt. A result without those details cannot be reproduced reliably.

Does your business actually match the buyer's question?

A query for an emergency plumber, a nationwide enterprise consultant, and a cheap local installer describes three different buying situations. Verify service category, geographic coverage, price tier, credentials, availability claims, and the exact customer problem. A company can be well indexed but a poor fit for the prompt. Do not call every omission an optimization failure.

Can a search system discover your strongest pages?

Check whether the homepage and relevant service pages return successful HTTP responses, are linked internally, and can be indexed where appropriate. Inspect robots directives, canonical tags, redirects, accidental noindex rules, and content rendered only after client-side JavaScript. Test the page as fetched HTML, not merely as it looks in your browser. Some AI products use third-party search indexes, so there is no single universal AI crawl test.

Do independent sources corroborate your claims?

Your website can claim to be the best; that is not independent evidence. Look for accurate industry directories, customer reviews, relevant professional associations, trustworthy coverage, case studies, and verifiable certifications. Correct stale listings before creating new ones. Avoid manufactured citations, fake reviews, and paid placements disguised as editorial endorsement.

Is the entity itself ambiguous?

If several businesses share your name, an AI answer may merge facts or select the wrong one. Make the legal and trading names, service area, leadership, contact details, and official URLs consistent. Organization and LocalBusiness structured data can describe those relationships, but markup is not a guarantee of recommendation. Confirm that any sameAs URL really represents the same entity.

How should you run a fair diagnostic?

Build 20 to 30 questions spanning branded, category, comparison, problem, and location intent. Include both favorable and unfavorable contexts, plus competitor-neutral questions. Run each under documented conditions, repeat samples because outputs vary, save full responses and cited URLs, and distinguish a mention from a recommendation or an actual source citation. A single screenshot is not a baseline.

What should you fix first?

Prioritize wrong business facts, inaccessible high-value pages, missing category explanations, and contradictory profiles. Then add original proof such as named case studies, transparent service scope, and verifiable qualifications. Recheck the same prompt set after sources are updated. The goal is accurate eligibility and stronger evidence, not manipulating a hidden ranking formula.

Can anyone guarantee ChatGPT placement?

No legitimate consultant controls ChatGPT's recommendations. Search behavior and answer composition can change without notice. Be wary of guarantees, secret submission portals, or promises that llms.txt alone will force citations. A useful engagement produces a reproducible baseline, a prioritized repair plan, and documented follow-up tests.
