Services: AI visibility first, SEO underneath, builds in the same shop

Gerris sells Chris Abraham’s expertise in one arena: how your business appears in AI search. The work is finding out what the answer engines say about you, fixing the sources those answers are built from, keeping your site indexed and readable underneath it, and building fast static websites for businesses that need one. Chris Abraham leads every engagement and is accountable for every deliverable. Every engagement starts in conversation, and commitments are in writing before work begins.

What is the AI Visibility Audit?

$750 flat. The audit runs a fixed set of 24 buyer questions, the questions buyers actually ask, in their words, across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, and Claude. We document what the AI answers say about you, where you are missing, and where a competitor is being named instead. We check the answers' sources against your homepage and up to five money pages, and your entity and name-address-phone consistency across your site, your Google Business Profile, and your directory listings. AI answers are assembled from those sources; when they disagree, the answer names the business whose facts line up. The audit covers one market: your home service area. The deliverable is a written report with every fix in priority order, delivered in five business days. It is yours to keep regardless of what follows.

What is the Claim Sprint?

The audit tells you what is broken. The sprint fixes the layer that fixes it:

  • Technical foundation: full crawl, Search Console review, titles and metas, broken links and redirects.
  • The claim layer: entity schema with sameAs links, an llms.txt file, a canonical page that states what your business is, and FAQ schema, so the machines can quote you instead of guessing at you.

Where an item belongs with your developers, we return it as a precise written fix list they can execute directly, and we verify it on the live site once they ship it. The sprint is scoped from the audit and costs $1,500 flat for the defined scope; work beyond that scope is priced in writing before it begins.

AI Presence is $500 a month, shaped around your search set.

Why does the Google Business Profile matter to AI answers?

For local and service businesses, the Google Business Profile is one of the primary sources AI answers are built from. A suspended, duplicate, or miscategorized profile damages your map listing and corrupts what the AI says about you everywhere else. Gerris profile work, including service-area configuration and reinstatement support, is fulfilled through the practice's Google Business Profile specialist partner and directed by Chris alongside the search work, so your profile, your website, and your listings tell one consistent story. That consistency is what the machines reward.

Why is Google not indexing my pages?

Indexing is where the repair work starts, because a page outside the index is invisible to search results and AI answers alike. Search Console states the symptoms in plain language: crawled, currently not indexed; discovered, currently not indexed; alternate page with proper canonical tag; soft 404. Each message has a cause: crawl budget spent on junk URLs, canonical signals that contradict each other, thin or duplicated templates, a sitemap advertising pages the site itself disowns, responses that waste the crawler’s visit. Chris reads the evidence in Search Console, traces the cause to the template, component, or setting behind it, and repairs the cause rather than resubmitting URLs and hoping.

The diagnosis comes first, inside the opening conversation, before any paid work: it is how Chris decides whether the repair can be made to work, and owners have never paid him for that read. Owners pay for the repair itself, and the ladder below is the published price list for it.
